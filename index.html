Welcome to CRYSTAL

Beauty, perfected.

A Sanctuary of Style in the Heart of Port Harcourt

At CRYSTAL Beauty Salon, every visit is an experience. Tucked inside on Odili Road, our studio blends warm hospitality with serious craft — a place where you can relax, be pampered, and leave feeling unmistakably you.

Our team of skilled stylists and technicians specialise in everything from intricate braids and flawless wig installation to sculpted nails, lash artistry, microblading and radiant makeup. Whatever your occasion, we bring precision, care and a personal touch to every appointment.
